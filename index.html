miku.gg Character Toolkit

Edit characters, move them between novels, and manage narration memories. Works offline in your browser: your files are never uploaded.

1
Load
Add one or two files below, or drop them onto the panels.
2
Work
Click Edit on a character to change its description, lorebooks, indicators and memories. Use Send to copy it to the other file.
3
Download
Press Download on each file you changed, then load it back into miku.gg. Your originals are never touched.
Which files work?
  • A novel file (exported from the novel builder): you can edit, send and delete characters, and edit their lorebooks and indicators.
  • A narration history (a novel that has been played): everything above, plus each character’s memories. Memories can be viewed, edited, deleted and sent along with a character.
  • For narrations, always use the “Download narration history” and “Load narration history” buttons in the narration window. Do not use the “Saved narrations” / “My narrations” tab: files from there are laid out differently and miku.gg will not open them again after editing.
Tip: keep your original files as a backup. This tool never changes the files you load; it only offers an edited copy to download.
